# Guard farmhand deletion with world maintenance

Farmhand deletion may be requested while people are playing, but it must execute only after the world rejects new connections and contains no connected human players. The default waiting deletion persists for at most 24 hours without occupying an active job; the explicit maintenance deletion warns every 10 seconds for one minute, remains cancelable through that countdown, then closes the join gate, disconnects players, saves and backs up before crossing the Junimo deletion boundary. Countdown cancellation and maintenance activation use a compare-and-set state transition so only one can win. Sleep or day settlement is latched as a safety cancellation until the Panel acknowledges it. Both saves are bound to the deletion operation and expected world. Failures before deletion reopen the world, while uncertain persistence after deletion leaves the join gate closed for administrator recovery. This adds durable intent and a bounded in-game maintenance lease because directly editing save XML or deleting against connected client snapshots would be harder to recover safely.
